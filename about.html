Our Story

Building the Future of Business, One Innovation at a Time

Founded in 2011 in Dubai, Stackly began as a small consultancy with a bold vision: to make world-class business solutions accessible to organizations of every size. What started with five passionate founders has grown into a global powerhouse of over 500 experts operating across 40 countries.

Over the past 15 years, we have guided hundreds of enterprises through digital transformations, operational overhauls, and market expansions. Our multidisciplinary teams combine deep industry knowledge with cutting-edge technical skills to deliver solutions that are both practical and transformative.

  • 500+ certified experts across 12 disciplines
  • Operations in 40+ countries worldwide
  • 96% client retention rate year over year
  • Winner of 30+ industry awards
  • ISO 27001, SOC 2, and GDPR compliant

Our Journey

Our Company Journey

From a five-person startup in Dubai to a global leader in business solutions — here are the milestones that define our story.

2011

The Beginning

Stackly was founded in Dubai with a team of 5 passionate entrepreneurs determined to make world-class business consulting accessible to companies of all sizes across the Middle East.

2014

Global Expansion

Expanded operations to 10 international markets across Asia, Europe, and North America, establishing partnerships with leading technology providers and growing the team to 120 professionals.

2017

AI-Powered Analytics

Launched our dedicated AI-powered analytics division, introducing machine learning-driven business intelligence tools that set a new standard for data-driven decision-making in the industry.

2020

500+ Enterprise Clients

Reached the milestone of serving over 500 enterprise clients worldwide, including Fortune 500 companies, while launching our proprietary cloud platform and cybersecurity practice.

2023

Global Recognition

Named "Top Business Solutions Provider" globally by leading industry analysts. Recognized for our commitment to innovation, client success, and excellence in digital transformation delivery.

2026

Leading the Future

Today, Stackly proudly serves over 1,500 clients across 40 countries with a team of 500+ experts. We continue to push the boundaries of what's possible in business transformation.
