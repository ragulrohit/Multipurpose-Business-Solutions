Who We Are

Building the Future of Business, Together

Founded in 2010, Stackly began as a small consultancy with a bold vision: to empower businesses of all sizes with the strategies, technology, and expertise they need to thrive in an ever-evolving marketplace.

Over the past decade and a half, we've grown from a passionate startup into a global force, serving over 200 clients across dozens of industries. Our team of experts brings together deep domain knowledge, technical excellence, and a relentless commitment to delivering measurable results.

We believe that every organization deserves access to world-class business solutions. That belief drives us to innovate, collaborate, and push boundaries every single day.

Our Journey

Company Milestones

From a small startup to a global business solutions provider, here are the key moments that defined our journey.

2010

The Beginning

Stackly was founded in New York with a team of five passionate consultants, a shared vision, and an unwavering belief that businesses deserve better solutions.

2015

Going Global

Expanded operations to Europe and Asia, opening offices in London and Singapore. Reached the milestone of serving 50+ clients worldwide and launched our Digital Transformation division.

2018

Technology Leadership

Launched our proprietary technology platform and AI-driven analytics suite. Named among the "Top 50 Business Consultancies" by Industry Insider magazine.

2021

Resilience & Growth

Helped over 100 businesses pivot and thrive during unprecedented times. Launched remote consulting solutions and grew our team to 150+ professionals across 12 countries.

2024

The Future is Now

Reached 500+ projects delivered and 200+ satisfied clients. Introduced next-generation AI consulting services and established strategic partnerships with global technology leaders.
